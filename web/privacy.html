Privacy

Your privacy

The short version: this site does not track you.

What we collect

Nothing on purpose. The site has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Third parties

  • The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs to run and protect its service.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them.
  • The contact form does not send anything to us. It opens a pre-filled GitHub issue, and nothing is published until you submit it on GitHub, under GitHub's own terms and privacy policy.

Questions
