What to look for

  • Before the window, the ECG is buried under the compression artifact. That is why the trusted check waits for the parked moment.
  • The amber band is the shock window. The piston is at full recoil the whole time it is open.
  • The bright amber line is the shock. Compressions restart right after it.
  • About twenty seconds later end-tidal CO2 jumps. The controller treats that as returning circulation, parks the piston and keeps breathing for the patient.
About this recording. The chest, the heart rhythm and the response to the shock come from a model. The controller, its limits and its decisions are the real code, the same code that is built for QNX. It is research software, not a medical device.
