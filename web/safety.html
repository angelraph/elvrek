Safety notice

Not a medical device

Please read this before you build, run or demonstrate Elvrek.

Intended use

Elvrek is research and teaching software for studying how a real-time controller for mechanical CPR can be built and tested. It is intended for engineers, researchers and trainers working with simulations, emulators and training manikins.

What it is not

  • It is not a medical device and has no regulatory clearance, approval or certification in any country.
  • It has not been validated for clinical use and must never be connected to, or used on, a person or an animal.
  • It cannot help in a real emergency. In an emergency, call your local emergency number and follow the dispatcher's instructions.

Bench and manikin work

  • Any physical build must keep a mechanical stop behind the software depth limit and a guard that keeps hands away from the piston.
  • The shock output must drive a lamp or an indicator only. Never connect it to a real defibrillator.
  • Always keep a hardware emergency stop within reach.

Known limitations

The rhythm checks do not yet meet the American Heart Association performance goals on real ECG. The piston, the chest and the patient's response are physics models. All results are reported with these limits in mind. See the evidence page for details.
