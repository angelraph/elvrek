Talk to us

Questions, bug reports, clinical feedback and offers of help all go to the same open place, so the answers help the next person too.

How to reach us

  • Questions and ideas: open a discussion or issue on GitHub. The form here prepares one for you.
  • Bugs and safety concerns: please describe what you ran and what you saw. Safety concerns are read first.
  • Clinicians and trainers: we would value your review of the rules and limits.
In an emergency, call your local emergency number. Elvrek is research software and cannot help in a real emergency.
