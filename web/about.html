About

Save lives, smarter

Elvrek started with one question: if a machine is already doing the compressions, why does the most dangerous pause in a resuscitation still depend on separate clocks and a tired pair of hands?

What we believe

  • Seconds matter more than features. The best thing a controller can do is keep the pause before a shock short and the compressions steady.
  • Smart parts must stay on a short leash. Learned models can help, but the operating system, not good intentions, should decide what they may touch.
  • Show the misses. A result that only lists passes is advertising. We publish where we fall short.
  • Open by default. The safety brain of a life support machine should be readable by anyone who has to trust it.
The project

Built for the QNX Global Hackathon

Elvrek was written for the Robotics and Medical category of the 2026 QNX Global Hackathon, using QNX SDP 8.0 through the free QNX Everywhere programme. It is built by an independent engineer, in the open.

Open source

Apache License 2.0. Every limit, rule and test is in the repository.

Built on QNX 8.0

Timer pulses, resource managers, abilities, security policy and launch monitoring, used natively.

Honest by design

No certification claims, no borrowed numbers, and results reported in ticks you can reproduce.
